# Editorial rules

Define particular senses, not whole words. Give positive content as well as contrasts. Explain relationship direction and conditions; allow overlap and contested interpretations. Mark analogy as analogy. Separate reality, experience, concepts, words, representations, and claims about them.

Every imported record must identify source, edition, location, license, attribution, modifications and review status. Public access does not imply redistribution permission. Do not import entire dictionaries merely because they are old or large. Coverage, sense quality, historical context and rights matter independently.

Current entries are original drafts prepared with AI assistance from the founding discussion. They are not copied dictionary definitions and have bounded AI-assisted source comparisons; independent specialist review is pending. Proposed definitions, relationships and examples may change. The user retains decisions on branding and licensing.

For each revision ask: What does this clarify? What does it obscure? For whom and in what context? What evidence would require revision? Keep distinctions between descriptive usage and proposed conceptual improvements visible.

See SOURCE_REVIEW.md for the scope of the initial comparison and MULTILINGUAL.md for language-specific interpretation rules.
