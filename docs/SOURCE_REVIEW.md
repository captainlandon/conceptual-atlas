## Walkthrough decisions

The examples illustrate distinct readings, borderline application, coexistence, pragmatic interpretation and uncertainty about an event. The “Fine” exercise no longer treats agreement, reluctance and reassurance as proof of multiple lexical senses. Multiple lenses can be selected. Intent or deception cannot be diagnosed from an unclear expression alone.
