# Conceptual Atlas charter

Originator: Anier Landon Woodyard. Working title. Updated mission recorded from the founding discussion, November 2026.

## Purpose

Build a durable, multilingual and cross-cultural atlas of humanity’s documented concepts, making their meanings, distinctions, relationships, contexts and limits explorable so people can better understand reality and lived experience, recognize the boundaries of their knowledge, discover unfamiliar possibilities and navigate life more effectively.

Dictionary reorganization is the foundation. The unit is a particular documented sense, connected to expressions and other senses through explained, scoped relationships. Multiple organizational routes coexist. Positive content, overlap, dependence, contested boundaries and cultural context remain visible.

## Goals

1. Organize historical and contemporary vocabulary by meaning and use while retaining ordinary lookup routes.
2. Help users recognize phenomena, distinguish nearby meanings, examine evidence and assumptions, ask better questions and revise interpretations.
3. Support learning, retention and transfer, testing these benefits rather than assuming them.
4. Offer an interactive digital resource, a self-contained offline edition and an independently navigable print edition from shared records.
5. Extend progressively across living, historical and signed languages and documented varieties, with appropriate speakers, scholars and knowledge holders involved. The present content pilot is English only.
6. Preserve original expressions, context and partial correspondences. A missing single-word translation does not establish a missing concept. English is not a universal standard.
7. Support personal discovery, useful connections and candidate conceptual development with prior-work searches and review.
8. Maintain sources, editorial status, attribution, rights, correction pathways, release history, accessible alternatives and portable data.

## Knowledge and ignorance

Known knowns: organize documented understanding and its grounds. Known unknowns: record unresolved questions, disagreement and missing evidence. Unknown unknowns: create opportunities for new awareness; do not claim an exhaustive inventory of what is wholly unknown. These states are relative to a person, community, task and time.

## Moonshots

Perceptible and explorable abstract structure; natural-process models with demonstrated structural fit; a consilient educational framework; useful conceptual invention; emergent knowledge architecture; a general epistemological instrument; contribution to human discernment, resilience, agency and flourishing. These guide research, not claims of achieved results.

## Current bounded work

Five English senses and five guided situations. Source comparisons are AI-assisted editorial work; independent specialist review and user evaluation are pending. The larger ambition does not require solving consciousness, proving that all knowledge is metaphor, or treating the atlas as reality itself.
